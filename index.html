Explore Biology

A collection of playful ways to explore biology.

The resources on this site are an expression of my vision of biology as a living science that you should interact with in order to understant. All ideas are my own. Game and apps are coded primarily by AI. My goal is to gain feedback on these so that I can apply for grant funding to hire a human developer to build versions of each app that can be shared broadly.
